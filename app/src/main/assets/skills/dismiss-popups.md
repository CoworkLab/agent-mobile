name: Dismiss popups and permission dialogs
description: Getting past a permission dialog, cookie banner, ad or rating prompt
apps:

A popup is an interruption, not part of the task. Deal with it the way a person would and
get back to the goal.

1. Read what the popup is asking before touching anything. The buttons are usually at the
   bottom, and the one that dismisses is often the quieter of the two.
2. Prefer the option that says Not now, Later, No thanks, Skip, Close, or the X in a corner.
3. Never agree to anything with a consequence just to get past it: no purchases, no
   subscriptions, no free trials, no changes to device settings the person did not ask for.
4. If the popup is asking for a permission the task genuinely needs, such as contacts for
   choosing a recipient, that is part of the task; approving it is expected.
5. If the popup asks for something that matters and the choice is not obvious, ask the person.

After dismissing, take a fresh look at the screen before deciding the next step. Dismissing
one popup sometimes reveals another behind it.
