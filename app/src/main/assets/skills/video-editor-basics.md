Editors differ in layout but the steps are the same.

1. Start a new project and pick the video from the gallery. Grant access to photos only if
   the task needs it.
2. The timeline runs along the bottom. Trimming means dragging one of its ends inward;
   splitting means placing the playhead and tapping a scissors or Split button.
3. To remove a section: split at both edges of the unwanted part, select that piece, then
   delete it. Deleting the wrong piece is easy, so check the selection is highlighted.
4. Music is usually under Audio or Music in the toolbar. Adding a track that is longer than
   the video is fine; the export will cut it.
5. Export is the final step. Choose a resolution the person asked for, otherwise keep the
   default. Exporting takes time, so wait for the progress bar rather than tapping again.
6. A share or save dialog usually follows the export. Saving to the gallery is safe; sending
   it anywhere is a separate step that needs approval.
