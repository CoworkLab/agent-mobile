---
name: Fill in a form
description: Entering text into several fields, such as a checkout or a sign-up page
apps:
version: 1
---

1. Look at the whole form first. Note which fields are required, usually marked with an
   asterisk, and what each one expects.
2. Work from the top down. Typing into a field can move the screen, so filling in order
   avoids losing your place.
3. Tap the field, then type. Use the field's own label or placeholder to be sure you have the
   right one, especially when two fields sit next to each other.
4. For a dropdown or a date, tap it and then choose from what appears. Do not type a date
   unless the field accepts typing.
5. Leave a field alone if you do not know the value. A wrong value is worse than a blank one,
   because it may be accepted silently.
6. Check the filled form by reading it back before submitting. Submitting is a separate step
   and needs the person's approval.
