Searching beats scrolling almost every time.

1. Look for a magnifying glass, or a field labelled Search. It is usually at the top.
2. If it is not visible, try swiping down from the top of the list, which reveals the search
   field in many apps.
3. Type a distinctive part of what you are looking for, not the whole phrase. "Amit" finds
   the contact; "message to Amit about the meeting" finds nothing.
4. Read the results and pick the match that fits. If two results look identical, ask the
   person which one they meant rather than guessing.
5. If the search finds nothing, clear it and try a shorter word before concluding the item
   does not exist.
