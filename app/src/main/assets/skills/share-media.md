---
name: Share a photo or file to another app
description: Sending media or a document from one app to another using the share sheet
apps:
version: 1
---

1. Open the item first, so it is on screen, then look for Share. It is often behind a
   three-dot menu or an icon at the top.
2. A share sheet appears with a list of apps. Pick the destination app.
3. Choose the recipient or the place to put it. This is the step where a wrong tap matters, so
   read the names carefully.
4. Add a message only if the person asked for one.
5. The send is a separate step. A prefilled share is not a sent one; the person approves the
   final send.

If the share sheet shows two accounts for the same app, ask which account to use.
